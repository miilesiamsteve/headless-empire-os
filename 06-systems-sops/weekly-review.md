# SOP: Weekly review

## Purpose

Close the week and set Big 3 for the next.

## Steps

1. Review TODAY files or calendar reality vs plan.  
2. Update project statuses.  
3. Clear or renegotiate waiting-for items.  
4. Log important decisions.  
5. Choose next week’s theme and Big 3 in `WEEK.md`.  
6. Schedule content commitments if marketing matters.  

## Output

Filled Friday review section + next week skeleton.
