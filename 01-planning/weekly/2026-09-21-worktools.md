## New custom-project ideas — pick one

These ideas were not in the reviewed project inventory. The finish goals are suggested scopes, not requirements from the linked tutorials.

- **Make a Scratch racing game.** Create a short race with controllable characters and a clear finish. Your first goal is a playable race that you can restart. Use the [official Scratch racing-game guide](https://scratch.mit.edu/help/studio/tips/howto/racegame-intro/). This is my first suggestion if you want a laptop project.
- **Build a cardboard marble maze.** Draw a route, add three obstacles and make a start and finish. Test it five times, then change one section that gets stuck. This is a good choice if you want to build with materials you already have.
- **Make a short stop-motion film.** Sketch a six-frame storyboard and animate a 20–30 second scene using small objects. Finish with a title and a beginning, middle and end.
- **Design a desk organiser.** Measure three things that need a home, sketch compartments and make a cardboard prototype or a CAD model. Test the fit before deciding whether to print or build a final version.
- **Build a reaction-time game, if you already have a micro:bit.** Follow the [official micro:bit reaction-game project](https://www.microbit.org/projects/make-it-code-it/reaction-game/), then test whether the game clearly identifies the winner. Check the required materials first; this plan does not assume you own them or need to buy anything.
